# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Semper contributors and maintainers must:

- Treat people with respect.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Accept clear, good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy and contributor attribution.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, project events, and private communication concerning Semper.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to lead maintainer Nihar Manchikakapudi using a private contact method listed on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

The lead maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a review is prohibited.
