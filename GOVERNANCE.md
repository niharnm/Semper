# Semper governance

## Project leadership

Nihar Manchikakapudi founded Semper and serves as its lead maintainer. The lead maintainer sets the project direction, manages releases, appoints additional maintainers, and makes final decisions when contributor agreement cannot be reached.

## Contributions

Anyone may propose issues, documentation, code, tests, or design changes. Maintainer status is earned through sustained, constructive work and may be granted or removed by the lead maintainer.

Every accepted contributor remains credited as the author of their work. Contributions do not transfer copyright to the project or its lead maintainer.
